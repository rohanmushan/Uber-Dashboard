# 🚕 Uber Ride Analytics Dashboard --- Power BI

> **An interactive business intelligence dashboard for analyzing Uber
> ride-booking performance, revenue, cancellations, vehicle behavior,
> demand patterns, payment preferences, and customer/driver
> experience.**

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Uber Ride Analytics Dashboard** is an end-to-end Power BI business
intelligence project built to transform raw ride-booking data into
actionable business insights.

The dashboard is designed from a **Data Analyst / Business Intelligence
perspective**, focusing on practical questions such as:

-   How many bookings were received and completed?
-   What percentage of bookings were successfully completed?
-   How much revenue was generated from completed rides?
-   Which vehicle types contribute the most revenue and booking volume?
-   Why are bookings being lost?
-   Are driver cancellations or customer cancellations the larger source
    of loss?
-   At what time of day is booking demand highest?
-   Which vehicle types have the highest lost-booking volume?
-   Which payment methods are most commonly used for completed rides?
-   Which pickup and drop locations generate the most bookings?
-   How does ride distance vary across vehicle types?
-   How do customer and driver ratings compare?

The final report is organized into **three analytical pages**, supported
by a dedicated Home/navigation page.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective is to provide a **single interactive analytical
view of Uber ride operations** covering:

-   Booking performance
-   Ride completion
-   Revenue performance
-   Fare behavior
-   Booking losses
-   Cancellation patterns
-   Vehicle performance
-   Time-based demand
-   Payment preferences
-   Customer and driver ratings

The dashboard converts operational booking data into a format suitable

# 🧮 DAX Measures

The report uses a dedicated **`_Measures`** table to organize business
calculations separately from the source data.

### Measures created

  Measure                        Analytical Purpose
  ------------------------------ -----------------------------------
  `Average Distance`             Average ride distance
  `Average Fare Completed`       Average fare for completed rides
  `Avg Fare`                     Overall average fare
  `Booking_Value`                Booking/revenue value calculation
  `Cancellation Rate`            Cancellation metric
  `Completed Revenue`            Revenue from completed rides
  `Completed Rides`              Count of completed rides
  `Completion Rate`              Percentage of bookings completed
  `Customer Cancellation Rate`   Customer cancellation percentage
  `Driver Cancellation Rate`     Driver cancellation percentage
  `Incomplete Rate`              Incomplete booking percentage
  `Lost Booking`                 Lost-booking calculation
  `No Driver Found Rate`         No-driver-found percentage
  `Overall Loss Rate`            Overall booking-loss percentage
  `Revenue per Ride`             Revenue per completed ride
  `Total Booking`                Total booking count
  `Total Lost Bookings`          Total lost bookings
  `Total_Distance`               Total ride distance

### Why a separate Measures table?

A dedicated measures table keeps the Power BI model organized and makes
the report easier to maintain:

``` text
Raw Data
   ↓
Calculated Measures
   ↓
Business KPIs
   ↓
Dashboard Visualizations
```

# 🔍 Key Business Insights

### Booking completion

Approximately **149K total bookings** were recorded, with around **93K
completed**, resulting in a **62.2% completion rate**.

### Booking losses

Approximately **56K--57K bookings** are represented as lost depending on
the report visual/measure context.

The major visible loss categories are:

-   Driver cancellations --- **27K**
-   Customer cancellations --- **11K**
-   No driver found --- **11K**
-   Incomplete bookings --- **9K**

### Cancellation behavior

The dashboard's largest individual loss-rate component is the **Driver
Cancellation Rate at 18.15%**.

Customer cancellation and no-driver-found rates are both approximately
**7.06%**, while the incomplete rate is **6.05%**.

### Revenue

Completed rides generated approximately **₹47.26M**, with an overall
average fare of approximately **₹508.18**.

### Vehicle performance

Auto, Bike, and Go Mini represent substantial booking and revenue
activity in the analyzed dataset, while Uber XL has comparatively lower
booking volume.

### Payment behavior

**UPI** is the largest payment method among completed rides, accounting
for approximately **44.95%**.

### Time-of-day demand

Q3 (**12:00--17:59**) and Q4 (**18:00--23:59**) show the highest booking
demand in the dashboard.
